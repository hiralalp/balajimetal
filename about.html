About Shree Balaji Metal and Alloys

The Total Industrial Raw Material Solutions.

Shree Balaji Metal and Alloys is one of the leading stockholders and suppliers of all kinds of stainless steel products. We are manufacturers, importers, exporters and stockists of stainless steel, carbon steel and alloy steel in the shape of pipes, tubes, round bars, flat bars, sheets, plates, wires, angles, channels, coils, strips and the entire range of pipeline fittings and flanges.

Over the last decade we have served many core industries across the country. Our quality standards follow accepted international standards, and we are ISO 9001:2015 certified. “No compromise in quality” is our motto.
