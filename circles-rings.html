Circles & Rings

Circles and rings are round blanks cut from sheet and plate. We supply them in stainless steel, alloy steel, carbon steel, boiler quality steel, high nickel alloys, duplex and super duplex, in thickness from 0.5 mm to 300 mm.

A circle is a solid disc, and a ring has a hole in the centre. Buying blanks saves on cutting, on handling full-size plates and on waste, since the supplier can nest your parts from a larger sheet. They are used as raw blanks for machining, pressing, spinning and fabrication.

Thin circles are usually sheared, blanked or laser cut, and they go into cookware, spun parts and lids. Thick circles and rings are cut by plasma, oxy-fuel or laser and go into flanges, tube sheets, pressure vessel parts, gear blanks and machine components. Cut edges can be machined where a closer tolerance is needed.

To quote, the important dimensions are the outside diameter, inside diameter, thickness and any machining allowance. For rings, it is worth comparing a ring cut from plate with a forged ring. A plate ring is usually economical for non-critical parts, while a forged ring has a better grain flow for highly stressed or pressure-retaining service.

Choose the grade by service and required standard. ASTM A240 grades suit corrosion resistant work, ASTM A387 and A204 suit high temperature pressure vessels, and A516, A515, IS 2062 and IS 2002 cover carbon steel and boiler quality needs.

When you enquire, please give the grade, diameter, thickness, quantity and whether test certificates are needed. If you have an unusual ring size or a nesting layout, send a drawing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a circle and a ring?

A circle is a solid disc. A ring has a central hole.

Can circles be supplied with a machined finish?

Please tell us the required tolerance and surface finish and we will confirm.

What is 15Mo3 and 16Mo3?

They are alloy steels used in boiler and pressure applications and can be supplied with IBR test certificates.
