Copper Wires

Copper wire is one of the most widely used conductors in industry. We supply copper wires up to 42 SWG in bright annealed condition and super enamelled copper wires up to 42 SWG, along with copper strips and sections to your requirements.

Copper is chosen because it combines high electrical and thermal conductivity with good ductility and resistance to corrosion. It can be drawn into very fine sizes, it solders and joins easily, and it holds up well in long-term service.

Bright annealed wire is drawn to size and then annealed in a controlled atmosphere. This softens the wire and leaves a clean, bright surface. It is easy to bend, wind and form, and suits conductors, braids, connections and general wire products.

Super enamelled wire, also called magnet or winding wire, has a thin, tough insulating coating. Because the film takes up so little space, many more turns can be packed into a coil. It is the standard wire for motors, transformers, relays, solenoids and other electrical windings.

Wire size is usually quoted in Standard Wire Gauge (SWG) or millimetres. Higher SWG numbers mean thinner wire. Selection should take into account the current, voltage drop, temperature rise and flexibility required. The table on this page gives the diameter, weight and approximate resistance for common sizes.

When you enquire, please state the type, size, temper, insulation class if enamelled, quantity and packing. We can also supply copper in rods up to 160 mm diameter and in strips and sections as per your specification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a higher SWG mean a thicker wire?

No. A higher SWG number means a thinner wire. For example, 42 SWG is about 0.102 mm, while 10 SWG is about 3.25 mm.

What is super enamelled wire used for?

It is used for windings in motors, transformers, relays and coils.

Which copper grade is best for electrical use?

ETP copper is the usual choice. OFHC is chosen for special high-reliability uses.

Do you supply by weight?

Copper wire is commonly sold by weight. Please tell us your total kg or length.
