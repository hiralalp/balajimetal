Ferrule Fittings

Ferrule fittings, also called compression tube fittings, join instrument and small-bore tubing without welding. We supply them in stainless steel, carbon steel, alloy steel, duplex and super duplex, nickel alloys, Monel, Inconel, Hastelloy, copper, brass, bronze and titanium.

A typical double ferrule fitting has four parts: a body, a nut, a front ferrule and a back ferrule. As the nut is tightened, the front ferrule seals against the body and the tube, and the back ferrule grips the tube. This forms a strong, leak-tight joint that can be made with hand tools.

These fittings are widely used on impulse lines, sampling systems, hydraulic and pneumatic lines, and analyser panels. Since there is no welding or hot work, they are quick to install, easy to take apart for maintenance, and useful where welding is difficult or not allowed.

A reliable joint depends on matching the tube to the fitting. The tube should be softer than the ferrule, with a clean, square-cut and deburred end, and with the correct outside diameter. Metric and inch tube sizes are close but not interchangeable, so always check which system your line uses.

The fitting range covers almost any routing need. Unions join two tubes, elbows and tees change direction or branch, male and female connectors link a tube to a threaded port, and manifold tees and port connectors tie several lines together. Pipe fittings such as nipples, plugs, bushings and caps complete the threaded side.

When you enquire, please state the type, tube size, thread type and size, material and quantity. If the system has a required working pressure, please share it so a suitable tube and fitting combination can be confirmed. This range is separate from the sanitary TC clamps and ferrules used on hygienic lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the same as a TC ferrule?

No. A TC ferrule is a sanitary welded end used with a clamp and gasket. These ferrules grip small-bore tube in a compression fitting.

Why use double ferrules?

The front ferrule seals while the back ferrule grips the tube, which gives a strong joint and helps resist vibration.

Can fittings be re-used?

Compression fittings can usually be disconnected and reconnected, subject to the maker's guidance.

Can I mix brands of ferrule and body?

This is generally not recommended. Keep parts from one fitting system together.
