Flat Bars

A flat bar is a long rectangular section with a width much greater than its thickness. We supply flat bars in stainless steel, duplex, alloy steel and carbon steel, as well as copper, brass, aluminium, Hastelloy, titanium, Monel, Inconel and nickel, to ASTM, AISI, ASME, DIN and UNS specifications.

Flat bars are among the most useful sections in fabrication. They are easy to cut, drill, bend and weld, and they are used for frames, braces, base plates, brackets, trims, wear strips and machine parts.

They can be made in several ways. Hot rolled flats have slightly rounded edges and a mill finish that suits structural work. Cold drawn flats have sharper edges, closer tolerances and a smoother surface for machined or visible parts. Sheared or cut flats are made from plate, and they are useful for sizes that are not standard.

Selecting a size is mostly a matter of strength and cost. Stiffness about the thin axis is low, so a flat bent on its wide side will flex more than one loaded on edge. Where this matters, consider a thicker flat or a different section such as an angle or channel.

Grade selection follows the same logic as other stainless products. 304 and 304L suit general work, 316 and 316L suit chlorides and chemicals, 310S and 321 suit high temperatures, and 410 and 420 are chosen for hardness. Duplex grades offer higher strength.

When you enquire, please send the grade, width, thickness, length, finish and quantity. Cut lengths can be supplied on request.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a flat bar and a strip?

Generally, strip is thin and supplied in coil, while a flat bar is thicker and supplied in straight lengths.

Can flat bars be polished?

Yes. Please state the finish you need, such as brushed or mirror.

Which flats are best for machining?

Cold drawn flats are usually preferred because of their closer tolerance. Free-machining grades such as 416 or 430F can also help.

Do you supply mild steel flats?

Yes. See our MS Structure page for patta, patti and other mild steel sections.
