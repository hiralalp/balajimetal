Forgings

A forging is a metal part shaped by compressing hot steel under a press or hammer. We supply forgings in stainless steel, duplex, carbon steel, alloy steel, nickel alloys, copper alloys, Hastelloy, Inconel, Monel and titanium.

Forging refines the structure of the metal and closes small internal voids. The grain follows the shape of the part, so the finished piece has better strength, toughness and resistance to fatigue and impact than a similar part simply cut from plate or cast in a mould. That makes forgings the normal choice for parts that carry pressure or high loads.

Forgings range from simple rounds, discs and blocks to rings and shafts, and to near-net shapes made in closed dies. After forging, parts are usually heat treated to the required condition and then rough or finish machined.

The material is selected for the service. Austenitic stainless steels suit corrosive conditions and clean processes. Duplex and super duplex give higher strength and resist chlorides. Carbon and low-temperature steels are common for general and cold service, chrome-moly alloy steels suit high-temperature plant, and nickel alloys and titanium serve the harshest conditions.

Quality is controlled through heat treatment records, chemical and mechanical testing, and non-destructive examination where the project requires it. If your job has a code, third-party inspection or special test requirements, please include them in your enquiry so they can be built into the quotation.

When you enquire, send the grade, standard, dimensions or drawing, quantity, heat treatment condition, machining level and inspection requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are forgings stronger than castings?

Generally yes, because forging refines the grain and closes internal voids. The exact strength depends on grade and heat treatment.

What is the difference between forgings and forged fittings?

Forgings are general forged parts made to your dimensions. Forged fittings are standard pipe parts such as elbows, tees and couplings.

Can you supply machined forgings?

Please tell us whether you need rough machined or finish machined parts, and send a drawing.
