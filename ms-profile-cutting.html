MS Profile Cutting

Profile cutting turns flat steel plate into finished shapes from a drawing. We supply mild steel profiles cut by CNC plasma, oxy-fuel and laser, in thickness from 2 mm to 300 mm, with plate sizes up to 6000 mm x 2500 mm or larger on request.

Instead of buying plate and cutting it in your own workshop, you send a drawing and receive ready-cut parts such as flanges, base plates, gussets, brackets, rings, sprockets and machine parts. This saves labour and reduces waste, and every piece in a batch is cut from the same program, so repeat parts match.

Each cutting method has its own strengths. Oxy-fuel burns through very thick plate at low cost, which makes it the normal choice for heavy sections. Plasma is faster on medium plate and offers a closer tolerance. Laser gives the finest detail and the closest tolerance, and it is best for thin and medium plate where accuracy matters.

The method affects the edge. All thermal cutting leaves a heat-affected zone along the edge, largest with oxy-fuel and smallest with laser. If the edge will be welded, the standard finish is usually fine. If it will be machined, leave a suitable allowance in your drawing.

Good drawings save time. Please send DXF or DWG files with material grade, thickness, quantity and any critical dimensions marked. Holes should normally be at least 1.5 times the plate thickness when cut by oxy-fuel or plasma. Smaller holes are better drilled after cutting.

The usual material is mild steel to IS 2062 Grade A or B, ASTM A36 or S235JR. Please tell us if you need test certificates or a different grade, and we will confirm availability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What file format do you need?

DXF or DWG is best because it can go straight to programming. A dimensioned PDF also works.

Which cutting method should I choose?

Generally laser for fine detail on thinner plate, plasma for medium plate, and oxy-fuel for thick plate. We can suggest one from your drawing.

What is the smallest hole you can cut?

For oxy-fuel and plasma, plan for a minimum of 1.5 times the plate thickness. Smaller holes are better drilled.

Can you cut other materials?

Please ask. Tell us the material and thickness and we will confirm.
