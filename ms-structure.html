MS Structure

MS structure is the general name for mild steel sections used in buildings, sheds, machine frames and fabrication. We supply angle, channel, beam, patta, patti, round bar, square bar and hex bar, along with bright round, square and hex bar, bright patta and patti, sheet piles and deformed rebar.

Mild steel is popular because it is strong enough for most structures, easy to cut and weld, widely available and low in cost. It does rust if unprotected, so sections are usually painted or galvanised when used outdoors or in damp conditions.

Each section has its job. Beams and channels carry bending loads in floors, roofs and frames. Angles work as bracing, purlins and connection pieces. Patta and patti, the common names for mild steel flats, are used for brackets, gates, grills and tie strips. Round, square and hex bars are used for shafts, fasteners and general fabrication.

Rebar is used to reinforce concrete. Its surface ribs lock it into the concrete, and its grade (Fe 415, Fe 500, Fe 550 or Fe 600) shows its minimum yield strength in MPa. Sheet piles are interlocking sections driven into the ground to retain soil or water in excavations, banks and waterfront works.

Bright bars start as hot rolled bars and are cold drawn or peeled to give a smooth surface and closer tolerance. They cost more than black bars, but they save on machining and suit precision parts such as shafts, pins and spindles.

When you enquire, please give the section, size, grade, length and quantity, and tell us whether you need bright or black material and any test certificates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are patta and patti?

They are common trade names for mild steel flats. Patta usually refers to wider flats and patti to narrower ones.

What does Fe 500 mean?

It is a rebar grade with a minimum yield strength of 500 MPa.

What is the difference between ISMB and ISMC?

ISMB is an Indian Standard medium-weight beam (I-section). ISMC is a medium-weight channel (C-section).
