Perforated Discs

A perforated disc is a round plate with a pattern of holes. We supply stainless steel perforated discs from 0.4 mm to 10 mm thick, in sizes from 100 mm to 1500 mm, cut from perforated sheet and finished to the diameter you need.

Discs are the basic part of many filters and strainers. Fitted across a pipe or vessel, a disc supports a finer screen, holds back larger solids, spreads the flow evenly or acts as a sieve plate. Thin discs are often stacked with mesh layers to build up a filter pack.

The disc is defined by its outside diameter, thickness, hole size, pitch and grade. Plan the pattern along with the disc size, because the holes should stop short of the edge if the disc will be clamped or welded into place. A solid rim makes it stronger and gives a clean sealing surface.

Thickness is usually set by the pressure across the disc and the unsupported diameter. A large disc under load will bend unless it is thick enough or supported by a grid. Reducing the open area also makes it stiffer, but it raises the pressure drop.

For corrosive or hygienic service, 316L is the usual choice. Grade 304 covers general filtration, while 310S and 321 are considered for high temperature use. Please tell us the temperature and fluid so that the right grade can be quoted.

When you enquire, send the diameter, thickness, grade, hole size, pitch, rim width, quantity and surface finish, or a drawing if you have one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I specify a disc?

Give the outside diameter, thickness, hole size, pitch, rim width, grade and quantity.

Can you combine a disc with mesh?

Multi-layer discs are commonly used for filters. Tell us the micron or mesh size and we will confirm what can be arranged.

What thickness do I need?

That depends on the pressure and the unsupported diameter. The equipment designer should confirm this for pressure applications.

Can discs have a centre hole?

Yes. Send a drawing with the bore size and any fixing holes.
